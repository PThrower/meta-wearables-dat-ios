## Slide 5: How It Works

Three pillars:

**1. AI Guidance & QA**
Computer vision watches the worker's actions in real-time. Provides step-by-step voice and visual guidance. Flags errors before they become defects.

**2. Hardware Abstraction**
One API across Meta, INMO, Mentra, Lensmoo, HeyCyan, and future hardware. Swap devices without changing workflows.

**3. Enterprise Integration**
Bi-directional sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, and logistics platforms. Site visits, inspection data, and task completion flow automatically.
